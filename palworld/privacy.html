Privacy Policy

Server Manager for Palworld (Unofficial) · Last updated: July 7, 2026

Short version: the app collects nothing about you.

Server Manager for Palworld (Unofficial) (the “App”) is a free desktop application published by Rhyse Gaming. It runs entirely on your own computer to install, configure, run, and back up a Palworld dedicated server. The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any analytics or advertising service. We operate no servers that receive your data, and there are no accounts, logins, tracking, or telemetry.

Information we collect

None. We (Rhyse Gaming) do not receive any data from the App. Everything you configure — server settings, profiles, backups, and the App’s activity log — is stored locally on your own device and is never sent to us.

Network connections the App makes

To do its job, the App connects to a few external services. These requests go directly from your computer to those services — not through us — and carry no personal information beyond what each request technically requires:

  • Your public IP address is looked up via ipify so the App can show you the address friends use to connect. This is only requested when you open the Connectivity page.
  • Discord — only if you choose to add a Discord webhook. If you do, the App posts server events (up/down/crash, player join/leave, backups) to the Discord channel you configured. This is entirely opt-in and goes to your own Discord.
  • App updates are checked and downloaded from GitHub (our release host).
  • The Palworld server files are downloaded and updated from Valve’s Steam / SteamCMD service, and the App may query steamcmd.net to see whether a newer server build is available.
  • Your local network router may be contacted over UPnP if you use the one-click port-forwarding helper. This stays on your local network.
  • Your local Palworld server is contacted on your own machine (its REST API and/or RCON) to show live status and let you manage players. This is local, machine-to-machine communication.

Each third-party service handles data under its own privacy policy — see ipify, Discord, GitHub, and Valve/Steam.

Data storage and security

All data the App creates — configuration, server profiles, world backups, and activity logs — is stored locally on your device (for example under your user’s application-data folder). You can view, export, or delete it at any time. Because nothing is sent to us, you remain in full control of your data.

Children’s privacy

The App is a server-administration tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
